# SpeakBuddy: 2–3 Minute Video Demo Script 🎙️

**Target Duration:** 2 minutes 30 seconds  
**Speaker:** Developer / Presenter  
**Audience:** Hacktoberfest Judges, DEV Community, Language Learners

---

### [0:00 – 0:25] The Hook & The Real Problem
**Action on screen:** Open `http://localhost:8000`. Show the landing page with the SpeakBuddy logo, "Start Practice" CTA, and scenario cards.

**Voiceover:**
> "Hi everyone! For the Hacktoberfest 2026 'Build for a Friend' challenge, I built **SpeakBuddy** for a friend of mine who is learning English. 
> 
> My friend understands English grammar when writing, but whenever they need to speak in an interview or casual conversation, they freeze up from fear of making mistakes. They don't always have a tutor around, and generic chatbots give long walls of text that don't help with spoken practice. 
> 
> SpeakBuddy is their personal, patient AI speaking partner that lets them practice speaking aloud anytime with zero judgment."

---

### [0:25 – 0:50] Scenario Selection & Starting Practice
**Action on screen:** Scroll down to the level selector, click "Intermediate", and then select the **Job Interview** scenario card. Click "Start Practice".

**Voiceover:**
> "Let's see how it works. SpeakBuddy offers 5 realistic scenarios: Daily Conversation, Job Interview, College Campus, Travel, and Confident Introduction. 
> 
> Today, let's practice for a **Job Interview**. I'll select Intermediate level and start the session.
> 
> Immediately, SpeakBuddy welcomes us: 
> *'Hello! Welcome to our interview today. To start off, could you please tell me a little bit about yourself?'*
> 
> Notice that we can also click the audio button to hear SpeakBuddy speak with natural pronunciation."

---

### [0:50 – 1:25] Voice Input, STT & Live AI Response
**Action on screen:** Click **🎙️ Speak**. The pulsing red recording indicator activates. Speak into the microphone:
> *"Hi, my name is Navya. I am studying computer science and I like programming."*
Click Stop. The transcribed text appears in the editable box. Click Send.

**Voiceover:**
> "Now I'll click the microphone button to answer aloud. 
> 
> *(Developer speaks: 'Hi, my name is Navya. I am studying computer science and I like programming.')*
> 
> The speech is converted to text instantly. If speech recognition misheard a word, I can easily edit it before sending. 
> 
> When I hit send, my response is processed by **Meta's Llama 3 8B model**, running completely locally on my laptop via Ollama. 
> 
> And look at that response! SpeakBuddy responds concisely like a real interviewer:
> *'Nice to meet you, Navya! It sounds like you have a strong interest in programming. What specific areas of computer science are you most passionate about?'*
> 
> It asked exactly one relevant follow-up question so I do most of the talking."

---

### [1:25 – 1:50] The Feedback Panel
**Action on screen:** Highlight the Speaking Feedback card beneath the user's message.

**Voiceover:**
> "Look at the gentle coaching panel right below my answer:
> 1. **Grammar Correction:** It suggests *'I enjoy programming'* instead of *'I like programming'*, explaining that 'enjoy' sounds more natural when discussing hobbies.
> 2. **Fluent Alternative:** It provides an idiomatic phrasing.
> 3. **Vocabulary Boost:** It introduces the word *'Passionate'* with a clear definition and everyday example.
> 4. **Confidence Tip:** An encouraging note reminding me that the interviewer wants to get to know my genuine interests.
> 
> No overwhelming grammar lectures — just simple, confidence-building tips."

---

### [1:50 – 2:10] Completing the Session & Summary Modal
**Action on screen:** Click **End Practice**. The celebratory confetti bursts on screen, displaying the Session Summary card.

**Voiceover:**
> "When my friend finishes practicing, they click **End Practice**. 
> 
> SpeakBuddy generates an encouraging wrap-up:
> - Shows conversation duration and total spoken responses.
> - An approximate practice indicator score to celebrate their effort today.
> - Specific highlights of what they did well.
> - Constructive areas to focus on next time, like verb tenses.
> - And all newly learned words are preserved in their personal vocabulary bank."

---

### [2:10 – 2:35] Progress Dashboard & Open Innovation Impact
**Action on screen:** Click "View Progress Dashboard". Show the metrics, session history table, and vocabulary bank.

**Voiceover:**
> "On the **Progress Dashboard**, my friend can track their practice consistency over weeks, see their top scenarios, and review their vocabulary bank.
> 
> Most importantly: **Why does open innovation matter here?**
> Because SpeakBuddy uses **Llama 3 8B and faster-whisper locally**, my friend's voice and thoughts never leave their laptop. They have 100% privacy, zero fear of data leakage, zero API subscription costs, and the freedom to practice anytime, anywhere.
> 
> SpeakBuddy helps my friend find their speaking voice. Thank you!"
